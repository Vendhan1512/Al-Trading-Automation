How changes are applied

  • Stricter changes (smaller size, slower, fewer assets, lower risk) are applied by the agent on-chain via tighten() — after the owner signs in.
  • Looser changes can only be signed by the owner's wallet (setMandate). The copilot never holds owner power.
  • What-if questions run the real Guardian in dry-run mode against live quotes. Nothing is ever signed.
  • Risk appetite: low = spread trades with ≥60 bps edge · medium = arbitrage + spread · high = + AI directional trades.

Reading this honestly

  • Prediction calibration (Brier score) is tracked separately from profit: accurate predictions can still lose money after fees and gas.
  • Net P&L includes gas paid by the agent, which the vault's cost basis does not.
  • The circuit breaker can only restrict: 3 losing exits in a row, ≥1 USDT drawdown or ≥50% failed transactions pause the vault; a Brier score above 0.3 disables AI-directional trades.
  • No claim of sustained profitability is made. The claim is that every trade's edge is checked, committed and provable.
